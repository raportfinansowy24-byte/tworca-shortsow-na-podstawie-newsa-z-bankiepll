# Finance Viral Shorts Strategy — RaportFinansowy24

Use this reference when creating Polish finance short-form videos for TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels.

## Non-negotiable opening rules

Choose one hook family for scene 1: **Contrarian Claim**, **Mistake Warning**, or **List Tease/Ranking**. Keep the spoken and on-screen hook to 10–14 words where possible, never above 14 words for the finance pipeline. Include a concrete number, percentage or amount verified in the source article. Make the first two seconds understandable with audio muted: the first frame must carry the hook visually and the captions must have high contrast.

Do not invent a number, guaranteed return, current promotion or investment recommendation. The proof scene must contain the source fact that supports the hook. Avoid repeating the same hook language more than a few times; use a new variant or formula while keeping the fact unchanged.

## Preferred 18-second structure

Use a 9:16 master with three editorial zones: `0–2s` hook, `2–14s` proof and useful explanation, `14–18s` payoff plus one clear CTA. The implementation may use more technical beats inside the proof zone, but the viewer should experience these three zones. The final CTA should point to `raport-finansowy24.pl` or the configured RaportFinansowy24 destination.

## Formula examples

Contrarian Claim: "Indeks rośnie, ale [spółka] traci [procent]."

Mistake Warning: "Stop: [spółka] traci [procent] — sprawdź, co się dzieje."

List Tease/Ranking: "Top 3 ruchy GPW: [spółka] traci [procent]."

These are patterns, not permission to make unsupported claims. Prefer a verified source fact over a stronger but unverified phrase.

## Platform adaptation

Render one clean 9:16 master for all three platforms. Use platform-specific captions and hashtags rather than baking platform-specific claims into the video. Track retention separately for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels; do not compare raw views across platforms.

## Metrics and iteration

Track retention at 1–3 seconds, average watch time, completion rate, comments per 1,000 views, shares, saves and link CTR. Treat 60%+ retention at 3 seconds as the initial target, not a guarantee. A hook with fewer than 30% early retention is a warning signal. Do not declare a winner from a tiny sample; keep one major variable constant per experiment and record `experiment_id` and `variant_id`.
